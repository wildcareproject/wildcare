The method

WILD:CARE is a way of attending to a place with other people. Four practices carry it.

W

Walk

Slowing down. Walking as an act of creation.

I

Inspire

The threshold between acting and receiving.

L

Listen

A receptive skill that can be learned. Listening makes space.

D

Design

Giving form to what was heard, within limits.

CARE is the field the four open onto: relation, responsibility, justice, healing.

The method starts from Deaf Gain. Listening happens through vibration, touch and sight as much as through the ear, and every exercise in the Kit is built that way.

Where it began

WILD:CARE grew from Passæggi Sonori, a participatory sound project by Æ (Aldo Scarpitta and Marco Berni), produced by Piccolo Teatro di Milano with Mare Culturale Urbano, Indagine Milano – Festival Immersioni 2025.
