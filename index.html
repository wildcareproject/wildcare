What this is

WILD:CARE is a sound-based participatory method for young people aged 18 to 30, piloted in Florence and Berlin.

Walk, Inspire, Listen, Design: CARE. The method is gathered in the Listening Kit: worksheets, a card deck and a recording app, free for youth workers to use. Co-funded by the European Union through Erasmus+, March 2026 to February 2027.

How the method works

Five ways in. Each word of the name asks something different of you.

WWalkto moveA chain of walks, one passing into the next.IInspireto receiveDraw one instruction from the Listening Kit.LListento listenA map of recordings, each one described in words.DDesignto makeCompose a score from seven pieces of the Kit.CCareto giveA soundscape that is always playing, made of the sounds people give it.
