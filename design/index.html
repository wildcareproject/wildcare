Seven pieces, seven worksheets. Each piece draws one sheet from its family in the Kit. Arrange them in any order: the score you leave can be read many ways. Tap a piece to read its sheet, and save the result as an image.

The pieces

    With a keyboard: Tab to a piece, move it with the arrow keys (with Shift for longer steps), R rotates, F flips. Double-tap a piece on the board to rotate it.
